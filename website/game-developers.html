Procedural game audio, SFX & adaptive stems

Infinite runs a sample-accurate, pull-based audio graph on a shared BPM transport. Instead of digging through static sample packs, build parametric sound recipes in .inf files or write per-sample DSP in Field Synth and Field Effect.

  • Physical & analog synths: Analog (PolyBLEP anti-aliased oscillators, unison, ZDF 4-pole Moog ladder and 2-pole SVF filters, 8-voice polyphony), Wavetable (12-table / 8-frame / 10-mip-level bank), FM, Metallic (modal resonator for hits, chimes, weapons), String, Piano and Wind.
  • Texture, foley & resynthesis: Granular, PaulStretch, Molder / Grain Molder (spectral resynthesis), onset-detecting Slicer, and Spectral Field (per-bin STFT phase vocoder).
  • Adaptive loops & spatial mix: 8-track Drum Sequencer (141 grooves, trig conditions and probability), 16-pad MPC, Spatial Mixer (3D headphone mix with head tracking) and 29 audio effects (Transient Shaper, Bitcrush, Pitch/Freq Shifters, Dynamics, Limiter, Reverb, Delay).
  • Zero-install browser generators: Generate procedural sound effects in SFX Generator or royalty-free loops/stems in any key and scale with the WASM-powered Sample Generator (-14 LUFS / -1 dBTP normalized WAV/FLAC/MP3 + .inf recipe).

Instant background ambient sound design (free with credit)

Need atmospheric menus, exploration beds or calm background music without licensing fees? Use Infinite's browser-ready generative engines directly in your personal or commercial game audio workflow.

  • Generative Piano & Ambient Engine (Nocturne): Physical-modeling piano (piano-engine.js), generative ambient synthesizer (ambient-engine.js) and procedural water audio (water-audio.js) for endless, non-repeating ambient soundscapes.

Procedural 3D, PBR materials & GLSL shaders

Author procedural geometry, tileable textures, flipbooks and shaders across 372 node types (macOS) on the same canvas as your audio—then export clean meshes, texture maps or compositing passes for your game engine.

  • 3D geometry & interchange: Import and process USD, OBJ, PLY, STL, glTF and GLB alongside primitives, 3D Text, Curves, Booleans, Voronoi/Delaunay, Decimate, Instancing on points (with GPU culling and point LOD), PBD cloth and soft bodies, Metaballs, Gerstner Ocean and Sketch 3D (JavaScript meshes).
  • Metal & OpenGL 3.2 Core / GLSL rendering: Live Metal engine on macOS (ray-traced shadows and reflections, GI + denoiser, up to 64 lights, MetalFX temporal upscale) + progressive Metal path tracer, plus an OpenGL 3.2 Core / GLSL 2D/shader pipeline with 32 blend modes, SDF shapes, reaction-diffusion and .cube LUTs.
  • PBR materials & engine render passes: Principled BSDF / Cook-Torrance GGX with 10 texture maps, parallax occlusion, true displacement, ACES/AgX tonemapping, 32-bit HDRI, and separate depth, normal, albedo, mask and id passes.
  • Field code domains & web tools: Write GLSL-style shaders in Field Pixel, parametric surfaces in Field Primitive and per-vertex deformations in Field Modifier. Try the browser 3D Logo & Text Loop Maker or inspect 145 standalone WebGL visualizers in Embeds (such as Chladni Cymatics, Cyber Tunnel and Metaball Lens).

Frequently asked questions

  • Are exported SFX, ambient beds, stems and 3D assets free for commercial games? Yes. Everything you synthesize or render in Infinite—desktop app, SFX Generator, WASM Sample Generator, or Generative Piano & Ambient Engine—is free for personal and commercial games with credit: "sample from Infinite by Naman Soni (n1m21n), infinitedevices.org".
  • Can I automate asset generation in CI? Yes. Use Infinite --validate, --frame, --render and --audio-summary in headless mode, or drive builds from Python with pip install infinite-daw.
  • Which platforms are supported? Native C++17/C++20 builds run on macOS, Windows (x64/ARM64) and Linux (x86_64 AppImage). Browser tools run directly in modern browsers.
  • What is the difference between the free app and the paid plugin? The standalone Infinite app (including CLI, Python SDK and MCP server) is 100% free and open source. The paid Infinite Instrument & FX VST3/AU plugin ($149, $99 launch) embeds Infinite inside external DAWs like Ableton Live, Logic Pro, Reaper and FL Studio.

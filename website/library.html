Download

Pick an item and press Download. You get one plain-text file: .inf for a template, .field for Pixel, .js for Sketch.

02

Import

Template: choose File, Open and pick the file. Pixel: add a Field Pixel node and press Import in its Devices row. Sketch: open a Sketch node's editor and paste the file.

03

Patch

Filters read an image you cable in. Generators draw on their own. Every knob can be modulated.

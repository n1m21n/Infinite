Install

From download to playing

  1. Download the installer for your computer and open it. That page shows the one-time step your system asks for.
  2. Open your DAW and rescan plugins.
  3. Add Infinite Instrument to an instrument track, or Infinite FX to an audio track, and open it.
  4. The licence screen opens. Copy the key from your email and click Paste key.
  5. Play a few notes.

From a terminal: infinite --install-plugins, and --uninstall-plugins to remove it.

Questions

My DAW does not list it

Rescan plugins first. Still missing? In Infinite, Settings, DAW plugin, press Copy path next to the folder, add that folder to your DAW's plugin folders, and rescan.

Does the app need to be open?

No. The plugin starts its own copy of Infinite in the background and closes it with your project.

macOS says it cannot be opened

Open Infinite from Applications once with right click, then Open. Install the plugin after that: the copy in your plugin folders no longer carries the download flag.

Windows protected your PC

SmartScreen stops apps it has not seen often. Choose More info, then Run anyway. You do this once.

How many computers?

Two that you own. One key file works on both.

Updating

Update the app and open it. The plugin follows. Close your DAW first so the old copy is not in use.

Lost key or refunds

Get your key emailed again. Refunds follow the refund policy.

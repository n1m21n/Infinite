Infinite v2.0.0

Latest 18 November 2026

Infinite 2.0 adds a 2D View for painting, layered compositing and adjustments, and it moves Render 3D onto a real-time Metal engine with a path tracer, richer materials, fog, sky and more lights. It adds a Field…
GitHub releasePermalink

Infinite 2.0 adds a 2D View for painting, layered compositing and adjustments, and it moves Render 3D onto a real-time Metal engine with a path tracer, richer materials, fog, sky and more lights. It adds a Field Patcher, a Spectral Field node, a Mixer and a hardware-style Drums panel, and a Session view that follows the Timeline. The release also widens the command line, headless and MCP surface, cleans up menus and shortcuts across panels, and changes the default save extension to .inf on every platform.

Timeline

  • Predictive Rhythm and Predictive Notes start from the block start and land on the next bar after a jump or loop lap; they learn looped takes in order.
  • Predictive Quantize runs on its own clock and resets after a long gap; the step clock stays idle while stopped.
  • The loop band is clipped to the visible timeline, and bars count from 1.
  • The drum sequencer plays the step under a jump.
  • The Timeline export can run headless from the command line (--render --timeline).

Session

  • The Session top bar matches the Timeline: Render Take, Audio, and the inspector toggle pinned right.
  • Session clips, scenes and drum lanes share one cell height.
  • Session clip menus hold their actions; values stay in Clip Settings.
  • Session clips take their source from the same Canvas Link pick as Piano Roll and 2D layers.

2D View

  • A new Scene 2D node and 2D View panel: a layered document with paint, vector, text and 3D layers, rendered as a node output.
  • Paint: a brush engine shared with the Draw node, paint layers, pixel masks, selection, bucket, gradient, eyedropper, stabiliser, and material brushes. Brush tip, grain and colour can come from a canvas node.
  • Retouch brushes: clone, heal, blur, sharpen, dodge and burn.
  • Vector and text layers: pen, node, shape and text tools; font search and preview; bold, italic, underline, all caps, justify, strike, kerning and baseline shift; Convert to shapes.
  • Layer effects: shadow, glow, overlay, stroke, inner glow, bevel and gradient overlay, in an effects stack that drags to reorder.
  • Adjustments: brightness, contrast, hue, saturation, exposure, invert, levels, curves, white balance, split toning, recolour, pixelate, halftone, dither, threshold, gradient map and posterize.
  • 3D layers: real 3D layers in the document, and paint on 3D object surfaces.
  • Transform: free transform, canvas rotate, liquify, distort, skew and perspective; scale with a lock; group scale and rotate.
  • Selection: expand, contract, smooth and border; antialiased magic wand and bucket edges.
  • Merge Down, Merge Layers (Cmd+E), Merge Visible (Cmd+Shift+E) and Flatten Image.
  • Layered PSD and ORA files open and save.
  • A canvas node can be added as a layer; media and Library items can be dropped on the canvas.
  • Layer numbers take modulators, and the brush follows them.
  • Faster redraws: plain layers composite in one batch, and an edit redraws only its layer and the ones above it. Brush latency is checked at 1080p, and tile PNGs encode off the main thread.
  • Photoshop-style shortcuts in the 2D View.
  • Redrawn tool icons, including the nib pen, bulb eyedropper, quick mask and selection operations.
  • Left, Right and Fill docks for the 2D and 3D Views.
  • An essentials-only tool strip with a tool picker window; the strip can be rearranged.
  • Layer copy, cut and paste; a canvas context menu; soft zoom with a typed zoom row.
  • The layer list scrolls, supports marquee selection and hits layers by their pixels.
  • Size presets, scale percent with a lock, and render format rows.
  • Gradient fills on vector shapes; conical, bitmap, mesh and noise fills; stroke miter, phase, arrowheads and pressure.
  • Layer shear, blend gamma and antialiasing; tool settings for gradient extend and dither, crop darken and reveal, selection brush add and subtract, and shape radius.
  • Pixel tools work on image layers, with the magic wand rule matched to Affinity.
  • The group eraser and retouch tools act on every paint layer in a group in one undo step.

3D View

  • Render 3D's Engine dropdown offers Live and Live + settle. Live draws in real time; settle refines to a path-traced still once the scene is still.
  • The Metal live engine (macOS) adds ray-traced shadows and reflections, global illumination with a denoiser, and up to 64 lights.
  • A progressive Metal path tracer with area lights and GGX materials. The commit measures it at 7.3 times faster per sample than the older GL tracer.
  • Materials: material layers with masks, parallax occlusion with self-shadow, true displacement in settle, curvature and cavity wear, dirt, dust and edge-wear recipes, eight surface recipes as Material presets, sheen fibre direction, hair, fur, subsurface and translucency, thin film, and exact Fresnel with multiscatter.
  • Shading nodes (Coord, Mapping, Checker, Noise, Voronoi, Gradient, Brick, Image, Bump, Normal Map, Fresnel, Layer Weight, Geometry) and value nodes (Math, Vector Math, Mix, Ramp, Map Range, Separate, Combine, Curves) compose into one material program. They sit in a new Shader category.
  • Sketch can drive a material map, and Sketch 3D mask() can drive a material attribute.
  • Light and air: a fog box with sun shafts, a physical sky on the HDRI node, and HDRI importance sampling on by default.
  • Camera and post: motion blur, cat-eye lens, lens distortion, chromatic aberration, depth-of-field bokeh, bloom, and AgX, Reinhard and Neutral tonemaps.
  • Render passes as Render 3D outputs: depth, normal, albedo, mask and id.
  • glTF KHR material extensions and texture transforms reach the Material.
  • Instanced batches with GPU culling and point LOD; Instance on Points uses the cloud transform and jitter.
  • MetalFX temporal upscale for interactive live frames at 67 percent. Exports stay native.
  • A failed engine pipeline shows as an error on the Render 3D node.
  • View and panel: Target X/Y/Z, background offset and grid axes on one row each; a background colour behind an HDRI world; Text 3D position, rotation, scale and spin can be keyed; Select Linked; Paste Transform greys out when nothing is copied.
  • Pose Mode and Sculpt Mode are Ctrl+Tab. Pose Mode greys out without a rig.
  • Viewport habits moved to Settings > 3D View.
  • The settle path denoiser, and export warm-ups that share one sun shadow mask.
  • Hair and fur: strand-to-strand shadows; live glitter path, settle coat and cutout shadows.
  • Live draws flat meshes two-sided, and the live and settled pictures match each other and Cycles at night.
  • Live: a ray-free variant for frames without a top-level acceleration structure, and a pure-live path that takes the HDRI sun out of its own map copy.
  • Field material programs get live knobs, and a shade texture can be written as Field Pixel code.

Node canvas and panels

  • Dropping a cable dims the nodes that do not fit and ranks the suggestions.
  • Any parameter can be reset to its default.
  • Cables inside a group's box can be hovered, selected and deleted; a node dropped on a cable is no longer pulled into a group it lands in.
  • Right-click menus are filled in: Cut on the canvas and Timeline, track Duplicate and Rename, and Curve Editor copy and paste of points.
  • Field Patcher: a box editor with drag-pan, copy, paste, duplicate and delete, a scrolling pick list, tab rename, dock switching, and twelve more starters. Pin and Pop Out sit at the right end of its strip.
  • Settings > Keys shows the same shortcut table the keys use.
  • Panel key tips match what the keys do; Cmd+R renames the page in Curve Editor, Field Patcher and Performance; empty states use one phrase.
  • Patches cook faster when they have many cables, modulators and expressions.
  • Canvas Link: one shared pick for Piano Roll, 2D layer sources and Session clips.
  • One page strip for Drums, Mixer, Piano Roll, Performance and Curve Editor; the same seven-row page menu in every panel; Cmd+R renames a page in Curve Editor, Field Patcher and Performance.
  • Pages save their order and colour; New Patch resets the Performance pages.
  • Right-click reaches every panel's menu, and right-click rows that did nothing now act or grey out.
  • Help lists the page and panel keys it had missed.
  • Sketch gains textFont, textAlign and fract.
  • Patches open in landscape layout by default.
  • Controls, chips and list rows share one 24 pt height, with three type sizes, two corner radii and one row gap of 3 everywhere.
  • Help and node display names agree; shortcuts respect the focused panel.
  • Sample Studio: Shift-drag stretches a slice and later slices ripple with it; the stretch border snaps to the grid with the magnet on; a cut slice keeps the settings of the one it came from; Space, Shift+D and Paste at Playhead work in the editor, and clicking a slice is silent.
  • Piano Roll: a slimmer clip header; right-click on it opens the clip menu, where Duplicate and Delete act on clips.
  • Context menus share one order across panels, with Settings as the last row under Delete.

Field

  • Spectral Field node: a Field kernel runs per bin (input bin) on an STFT engine with a phase vocoder.
  • Field Patcher panel: a box graph with pages, a canvas, a pick menu, an inspector, search, templates, starters and a Value mode. Sugar boxes expand and reopen as the box. Scratch pages are saved in the patch.
  • Field Pixel programs can drive materials (Material domain) in Render 3D, including material layers.
  • Field Notes: scratch pages with a value or pulse outlet export to Field Notes behind the publish-scalar toggle.
  • New noise built-ins in Field: noise3, noise4, fbm, voronoi3, wave, brick, checker, gradient and white.

Audio and DSP

  • Generate Samples gains new music styles: five distinct styles, then microtonal ambient, prepared piano, Berlin School, UK dub steppers, and four more.
  • Lower CPU on the audio thread: dB-to-linear is memoised on settled gain knobs; Chorus, Flanger, Phaser and Frequency Shifter use a polynomial LFO sine; Wavetable Shaper hoists its lookups; dry processing is skipped at full mix.

Mixer and Drums

  • Mixer panel: 32 strips with trim, polarity, solo-safe and a pan law; five docks; a console with pages, groups and master; Mixer Audio mode (Shift+L); Open in Mixer panel from the node.
  • Mixer sends and returns; inserts on strips and on the master; master loudness; snapshots; mono and dim; a render column and Render All.
  • Mixer hardware-desk look that follows the theme.
  • Drums panel: the hardware face of the Drum Sequencer with lanes, a Step page, a FILL key, an LCD, per-lane length, reverse and filter. Two-tier Drumazon layout. One undo step per trim drag.
  • Drum Sequencer: trig conditions, probability, nudge, FILL, per-lane length, reverse and filter. Audition fires a lane without starting the transport.
  • Drum kits per groove, built from the user's own sample library.
  • Mixer and Drums share a chip header, flat wells, and docked card styling; the Drums panel has its own rail icon.
  • The Mixer empty state offers New Mixer.

Terminal, CLI, headless and MCP

  • CLI: --frame with --batch; alpha kept with --size; --render --timeline; view2d RPC and CLI commands.
  • CLI and RPC verbs: view2d-add; Timeline clip copy, paste, split at playhead, duplicate, inspect and marker colour; node drum and node mixer for the Drums and Mixer panel menus.
  • Headless jobs are isolated and deterministic; headless runs wait for sample analysis.
  • RPC: bind_mod, unbind_mod and set_expr accept more argument forms; the transport loop action; per-parameter reset; cook time and fps over RPC; a bad field returns an error.
  • Agents: a propose-agent gate validates schema and code, with rollback.
  • Chat: a built-in OpenAI-compatible custom agent; /model shorthands; the agy model list is parsed and its errors shown.
  • MCP picture tools: compare frames, find a knob, and inspect node effects; colour nodes are included.
  • OSC: recursive bundles, T/F/d/h type tags, and * and ? address matching.
  • The command-line reference, headless docs and AI skills were regenerated for the new panels and verbs.
  • Prediction nodes (Drift, Moves, Predictive Notes, Rhythm, Quantize, Coloring, Velocity) carry a profile_dependent flag in node descriptions, because headless runs do not load their learned profiles.

Plugins

  • On the website, the plugin and the free-app buttons sit under Download in the hero. The footer and mobile dock no longer link to the plugin page.

  • Help tooltips inside nodes no longer blank node rows.
  • The node editor no longer aborts when a right-click lands during a panel splitter change.
  • The 3D View no longer crashes drawing a scene before its first sync.
  • Image export no longer turns paint tiles upside down.

  • Predictive timing: Vmpc seek-back fixed.
  • Render 3D: grazing-angle sun glint, and thin glass and water are no longer stepped over by settle rays.
  • Mixer M/S state fixed; drum rows keep their size.
  • Pages: New Patch resets the Performance pages.

  • An empty text font falls back to the default sans.

  • Cables: a plain picture is refused on a Shade field pin instead of connecting and doing nothing.

Upgrading from 1.x

  • Save extension: patches save as .inf on every platform. .infinite files still open.
  • Default changes that alter how old patches render or play:
    • Render 3D: HDRI importance sampling is on by default; settled pictures can differ from 1.2.0.
    • Render 3D settle: adaptive stop is on by default at 0.05, and settle uses new Sobol samples and ray offsets. Settled pixels change even when the patch is unchanged.
    • Render 3D: sheen and tonemap are computed from one source, and Fresnel and multiscatter gain are exact on raster and live. Expect small shading differences.
    • Shortcuts: Cmd+0, Cmd+=, Cmd+- zoom the View under the pointer; Pose and Sculpt Mode are Ctrl+Tab; Cmd+E merges layers; Cmd+R renames pages only in Curve Editor, Field Patcher and Performance.
    • Plugin nodes declare machine-dependent and inactive parameters; a plugin must be installed on the machine that opens the patch.
  • Opening files: the patch header is infinite-patch 1 in both 1.2.0 and 2.0 (kVersion in src/core/Patch.cpp is unchanged). 2.0 reads 1.x patches. The loader ignores lines it does not know, so a 1.2.0 build does not reject a 2.0 file on version grounds. Whether a 1.2.0 build loads 2.0-only node types (Scene 2D, Spectral Field, Mixer, Drums, Field Patcher pages) correctly is untested.
  • Whether 1.2.0 shows or opens .inf files in its file dialog is untested.

Known issues

  • Live engine: a commit records a known gap in the grazing-angle sun glint. A later commit (6dc0f44f) adds a glint fix; no commit records whether the gap is closed.
  • Three render goldens differ slightly from their baselines (3D render shading by about 1/255; voice spread +1.3 dB; envelope per voice +1.05 dB). Not bisected; fix planned for 2.0.1. Details in docs/release/known-issues-2.0.md.
  • Convert to shapes on Windows gives a thinned outline, not the font's Bezier curves.
  • The Vulkan backend is not in this build (INFINITE_VULKAN is OFF by default). The Render 3D live and settle engines are Metal, so the commits do not confirm them on Windows or Linux.

Infinite 1.2 runs inside your DAW. Infinite Instrument and Infinite FX are VST3 plugins on macOS, Windows and Linux, and Audio Units on macOS, with your whole patch saved in the DAW project. The plugin is paid: $149 one time, $99 at launch, for two computers, with every update for life. The standalone app stays free and open source, and this release brings it fixes too.

Infinite as a plugin

  • Two plugins from one app: Infinite Instrument takes the track's MIDI, Infinite FX processes the track's audio. Each opens the full Infinite editor inside the DAW
  • Saved with your project: the patch, its settings and its host automation come back when the project reopens, even before the engine starts
  • Host automation: right-click any knob or parameter and choose Host automation. It appears in the DAW under the node's own name. Up to 128 per instance, and none show until you bind one
  • DAW sync (Settings): choose whether the DAW or Infinite owns tempo, playhead and loop. Play and stop always follow the DAW
  • MIDI from the DAW: the track's MIDI reaches every MIDI node, and CC bindings learned on hardware follow it; Sample Studio records the DAW track
  • Pop out: move the editor into its own window, and back. Projector windows open on real displays from inside the DAW
  • Safe when things go wrong: if the engine crashes or hangs, the DAW keeps running, the plugin goes silent and Restart brings back the patch that was on screen
  • Inside a DAW, Infinite hides what does not belong there: audio device settings, its own play and rewind, updates, offline renders and recording
  • Licence: paste the key from your email once and it works offline in every DAW on that computer
  • Installers: Infinite.pkg on macOS and Infinite-Setup-windows-x64.exe on Windows let you tick the app, VST3 and AU; on Linux, install.sh does the same with no sudo. Settings > Infinite as VST3/AU in the app copies or updates the plugins too

Curve Editor

  • A docked panel for drawing curves: open it from the rail or with Shift+E. Every curve is an MSEG page, and you can dock the panel on any side
  • Draw, then assign: draw a curve, press Assign and click any knob in the patch, sound, picture or 3D. One curve can drive many knobs, each with its own range
  • Tools: select, pan, pen with bezier handles, freehand draw, line, stamp, bend, eraser, transform and smooth, plus reverse, invert, mirror, simplify, humanize and quantise
  • Shapes: waves, gates and rhythms, envelopes, risers, drops and sidechain pumps, random, and 38 motion easings (Penner, CSS, Material). Save your own
  • Type a formula: write f(t), see it as a curve right away, and Bake it into editable points
  • Record a move: arm Record, then move a MIDI CC or a knob. The take becomes a short editable curve instead of a raw recording
  • Time: set the length in beats, seconds or frames, with grid, snap and quantise. Play it once, looped or ping-pong, and crop to a loop section
  • 3D motion: bezier keys with Blender's handle types, and Position, Rotation and Scale groups that fill three linked curves for a 3D node. Frame-exact at 24, 30 and 60 fps
  • Your old patches: existing MSEG nodes open and sound the same

App

  • Output window: a Transparent window toggle, so OBS window capture can key it (#26)
  • Wiring: a node added from a dragged pin is wired to it, and search lists only nodes that fit that pin (#48, #52)
  • Stutter: every repeat edge is faded, so loops and mutes no longer click (#51)
  • Shadertoy: pasted #version and #extension lines no longer break the compile (#50)
  • Modulator meter: samples on wall-clock time and restarts cleanly after being off-screen (#49)
  • Agents: stage-by-stage setup check-ins per agent and OS, verified install folders, and the API-key step as the agent's own terminal command (#47)
  • Linux file dialogs: kdialog on KDE, and GTK4 zenity dialogs forced to cairo so they draw (#45)
  • Memory: patches without a Wavetable no longer grow by about 1 MB a minute
  • Terminal: infinite node <node> window transparent

Infinite v1.1.0

5 November 2026

Infinite 1.1 turns the 3D View into a full workspace: Edit Mode, Pose mode, sculpting, baking, rigging, materials and a path-trace engine, with five new nodes. It also adds a Chat panel that works with your own Claude,…
GitHub releasePermalink

Infinite 1.1 turns the 3D View into a full workspace: Edit Mode, Pose mode, sculpting, baking, rigging, materials and a path-trace engine, with five new nodes. It also adds a Chat panel that works with your own Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini, and native ASIO audio on Windows.

3D View

  • Edit Mode (Tab): select, move, rotate, scale, extrude, inset and delete on a mesh, kept by an Edit Mesh node so the edit replays on the input. G, R and S take X, Y and Z (Z is vertical, as in object mode) and Shift+axis locks to a plane, with a readout in the displayed axis order. Edits keep their meaning when the input mesh is refined
  • Pose mode with weight paint, and a Material tab that edits the selected object's Material and its maps, with a live preview ball
  • Shading and overlays: Solid view with object colour, X-ray (Alt+Z) and a wireframe overlay, per-item shadow casting, flat meshes drawn on both sides, and a grid that depth-tests correctly: it no longer bleeds through meshes, and the mesh wins where it meets the floor
  • Render passes: mist (with a range display on the camera), object index and cavity; motion blur; a camera background image; a render region; a render-time stamp and a Finish for renders
  • Cameras and lenses: cylindrical 360 and custom fisheye lenses, camera sensor presets, and leaving camera view returns you to the view you had before
  • Selecting and moving: circle select, trackball rotate, persistent rulers, continuous grab, centre view to mouse, Alt-drag box select in the outliner, outliner reorder, filters and keyboard walk, and Shift+double-click on empty space to glide the pivot back to the 3D cursor
  • Placing things: drop a model file into the view, turn any 2D output into a textured picture plane, motion-modulated placements and Follow, and a right-click menu that changes with what you click
  • Units, preferences and operators for transform, snap, placement and the object, select and view operators, with the parity checked row by row against Blender's 3D View. The nav and edit axes are self-tested
  • Trackpad: Shift pans, Ctrl or Cmd zooms and plain scroll orbits, undoing macOS's Shift scroll swap
  • Edit Mesh, Sculpt and Armature cards have an Open in 3D View button; the scene list uses item keys instead of action buttons

New nodes

  • Sculpt: brushes with strengths and spacing fitted to Blender's own (draw, inflate, flatten, pinch, crease), symmetry that follows the displayed axes, any 2D node as the brush image, and strokes whose dabs land on the surface where the stroke began
  • Bake: a normal map from a sculpt
  • UV Project: projects UVs onto any mesh
  • Edit Mesh: the Edit Mode operations as a node; it passes its input through when bypassed
  • Armature: bends any geometry with a bone chain, with weight paint

Rigging and models

  • Skinned glTF clips and morphs in Model 3D, fitted to the rest pose, with IK driven by 3D View items
  • glTF import: one part per material, file textures right side up, and spec-gloss materials read (diffuse map, roughness from glossiness, specular from F0)
  • Text 3D: even caps and banded walls, so sculpts and modifiers stop tearing letters

Materials and Render 3D

  • Path-trace engine: a progressive path tracer with a BVH, with clearcoat, sheen, glass (IOR, rough transmission), specular level, depth of field matching the raster's blur, material texture maps (albedo, roughness, metallic, normal or bump, emission, clear coat, sheen, transmission), opacity with an alpha cutoff and opacity map, and refinement in frame-budget strips. Choose it from the Engine dropdown on the Render tab; raster-only rows go grey under it. Use it for scenes that rely on global illumination
  • Raster: soft area-light shadows that follow the light's size, a floor bounce, Blender's spot-light cone falloff, light falloff, Ambient, diffuse and specular, the exact sRGB curve instead of gamma 2.2, and a sheen visibility term
  • Materials: 12 measured Material presets, opacity and transmission maps, a Load map field that spawns a cabled Image Source, and images that can be dropped onto a Material. Controls the path tracer ignores are greyed
  • Render 3D has balanced columns and a node-wide show more or show less

Physics and modelling

  • Cloth: bending, a collider mesh input with Coulomb friction (0 to 10), real-world gravity that pulls every mass the same, and a draped-over-a-ball check. Cloth no longer bypasses: it has two inputs, so an old patch with a bypassed Cloth behaves differently
  • Rigid Bodies: a box uses the piece's own axes, with warm-started contacts
  • Subdivide is Catmull-Clark on recovered quads, to the limit surface, and faster. Twist on Y turns the same way as X and Z
  • Sculpting is incremental, and a 3D bench measures the slow paths

Chat

  • A Chat panel where you talk to your own Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini. Infinite ships no agent: Settings > Agents installs and signs in the ones you choose, and a first-run flow guides someone who has never used a terminal. A missing Node is said once, with its real reason
  • Type @ to name a node, / for commands, @gemini or @chatgpt to switch agent; one model picker for all agents; queue messages while an agent works
  • Pictures, files, audio, video and animated gifs in the thread; paste pictures and files; code opens in a side pane
  • A change card shows what the agent changed, with live knobs and the agent's value as a dot (double-click to put it back)
  • Agents can find nodes by plain words, import files, generate samples and MIDI, and link every settings page
  • Pop the chat out into a floating window; token use per turn and in Settings
  • Node menu: Reference in chat
  • Every chat string is translated into Spanish, German, Chinese, Japanese and Russian

Windows audio

  • Native ASIO: choose ASIO as the output mode in Settings > Audio, pick your interface's driver and play at buffer sizes down to 32 frames, with a round-trip latency readout.

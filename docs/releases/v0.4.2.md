## What's new in v0.4.2

### Features

- **A new Prediction category — Infinite now learns how you move.** Four new nodes: **Predictive LFO** (wanders the way your hand tends to move a control, with Speed, Low/High range, Stray, and Momentum), **Predictive Modulator** (learns the shape of whatever you patch into it, then keeps playing it after you stop teaching), **Predictive Notes**, and **Predictive Macro** (macro faders learned from your multi-knob gestures, so a few faders drive many coupled parameters the way you would). Predicted values show as a ghost line on the control they drive, with a confidence dot, and Shift-grab takes a control back instantly.
- **It learns *you*, not whatever was left running.** Your hand movements, exploratory play, automation, and replays are weighted separately, so an overnight LFO no longer drowns out a thirty-second performance.
- **Per-clip modulation bypass** on the timeline — switch off individual modulators on a single clip from Clip Settings.
- **Make Unique** for arrangement clips that share a source, and the shared-source warning is now a single line.
- **Modulation Matrix** now shows color-coded category dots and live sparklines.
- **Analog synth tuning header** now matches Oscillator and Wavetable: a fine-tune slider plus octave and semitone dropdowns per oscillator.
- **Comb filter type** added to the synth and generator nodes.
- **Better Random Note and Chorder algorithms**, and Chorder's rate now uses the standard musical divisions.

### Fixes

- Fixed **Transform** (and the other geometry operators) silently dropping point clouds and curves, so *Mesh to Points → Transform → Render 3D* rendered nothing.
- Fixed trimming the start of a timeline clip leaving its source offset out of sync.
- Fixed clip inspector fields on the timeline not typing like the canvas ones, in their own units.
- Fixed 3D text glyph counters (holes in letters like *o* and *e*) self-intersecting.
- Fixed Spout receive showing black on GPUs without interop.
- Fixed **MIDI CC from 14-bit controllers** such as the Pioneer DDJ-FLX4 jumping erratically and not following the knob (coarse and fine bytes are now paired).
- Fixed double-clicking a slider or knob to type a value sometimes snapping it to a rounded number (often 0) if the field closed without typing.
- Fixed typing a value into a double-clicked slider while the mouse rests over a different param, which stole the keystrokes and left the field you clicked at its minimum.
- Fixed Video In crashing on Windows when picking a fixed resolution (e.g. 1080p) on a capture card or camera whose current size differs.
- Fixed **Predictive Modulator** bindings showing amber like a regular modulator; they now read green like the rest of the Prediction category (knob, cable, Modulation Matrix and preview).
- Predictive LFO and Predictive Macro can no longer be cabled into other nodes (Smooth, Math, ...) - they only drive parameters - and the refused drop now says why.
- Fixed long status text (like an audio input device error) running out past the edge of audio nodes.
- Fixed a Linux build error, so the AppImage builds again.

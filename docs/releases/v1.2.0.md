## What's new in v1.2.0

Infinite 1.2 runs inside your DAW. **Infinite Instrument** and **Infinite FX** are VST3 plugins on macOS, Windows and Linux, and Audio Units on macOS, with your whole patch saved in the DAW project. The plugin is paid: $149 one time, $99 at launch, for two computers, with every update for life. The standalone app stays free and open source, and this release brings it fixes too.

### Infinite as a plugin

- **Two plugins from one app:** Infinite Instrument takes the track's MIDI, Infinite FX processes the track's audio. Each opens the full Infinite editor inside the DAW
- **Saved with your project:** the patch, its settings and its host automation come back when the project reopens, even before the engine starts
- **Host automation:** right-click any knob or parameter and choose **Host automation**. It appears in the DAW under the node's own name. Up to 128 per instance, and none show until you bind one
- **DAW sync (Settings):** choose whether the DAW or Infinite owns tempo, playhead and loop. Play and stop always follow the DAW
- **MIDI from the DAW:** the track's MIDI reaches every MIDI node, and CC bindings learned on hardware follow it; Sample Studio records the DAW track
- **Pop out:** move the editor into its own window, and back. Projector windows open on real displays from inside the DAW
- **Safe when things go wrong:** if the engine crashes or hangs, the DAW keeps running, the plugin goes silent and **Restart** brings back the patch that was on screen
- **Inside a DAW, Infinite hides what does not belong there:** audio device settings, its own play and rewind, updates, offline renders and recording
- **Licence:** paste the key from your email once and it works offline in every DAW on that computer
- **Installers:** `Infinite.pkg` on macOS and `Infinite-Setup-windows-x64.exe` on Windows let you tick the app, VST3 and AU; on Linux, `install.sh` does the same with no sudo. **Settings > DAW plugin** in the app copies or updates the plugins too

### App

- **Output window:** a **Transparent window** toggle, so OBS window capture can key it (#26)
- **Wiring:** a node added from a dragged pin is wired to it, and search lists only nodes that fit that pin (#48, #52)
- **Stutter:** every repeat edge is faded, so loops and mutes no longer click (#51)
- **Shadertoy:** pasted `#version` and `#extension` lines no longer break the compile (#50)
- **Modulator meter:** samples on wall-clock time and restarts cleanly after being off-screen (#49)
- **Agents:** stage-by-stage setup check-ins per agent and OS, verified install folders, and the API-key step as the agent's own terminal command (#47)
- **Linux file dialogs:** kdialog on KDE, and GTK4 zenity dialogs forced to cairo so they draw (#45)
- **Memory:** patches without a Wavetable no longer grow by about 1 MB a minute
- **Terminal:** `infinite node <node> window transparent`

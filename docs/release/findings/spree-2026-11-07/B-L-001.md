## What happened

GeometryNode::RebuildIfNeeded used detail, sides, bevelSegments, knotP and knotQ as loaded. At 1e9 the main thread allocated until the 8 GB Mac swapped to a stop (no crash, no report).

## Fix

Clamp at the top of RebuildIfNeeded: detail 1..512, sides 3..512, bevel 1..16, knotP/Q 1..64. Saved patches use at most 96 / 64 / 1 / 6 / 3, so none change.

## Repro

1. Write a patch with a Chorder whose params hold `i root 2147483647` (or `i scale`, `i chordSize`), and load it. The same goes for Note Filter and Random Note Generator (`root`, `scale`, `rangeLow`, `rangeHigh`) and Keyboard (`baseOctave`, `transpose`).
2. Or open the fuzz cases `int-Chorder-*.inf`, `int-Keyboard-*.inf`, `int-Note_Filter-*.inf` and `int-Random_Note_Generator-*.inf` under the sanitizer build.

## Expected

The node loads with a usable key and range, and nothing reads outside its tables.

## Actual

- **Chorder:** the process dies with SIGBUS (rc -10) at INT_MAX and at 100000. The body indexes `NoteNameList()[n->root]` (NoteBodies.cpp:2121), and the voicing math overflows.
- **Note Filter, Random Note Generator:** the bodies index NoteNameList with a negative `range % 12`, and `midiNote - root` overflows in SnapToScale.
- **Keyboard:** `(baseOctave + 1) * 12` overflows (NoteBodies.cpp:593).

Patch::LoadParams passes ints through unchecked. The UI dropdowns and knobs can't produce these values, so only a damaged or hand-edited patch reaches them.

## Fix note

Each node clamps after loading:
- `root` wraps to its pitch class (14 becomes 2).
- An unknown `scale` plays as major, the same fallback MusicTime::ScaleTable already used.
- Ranges go to 0..127, Chorder's `chordSize` to 2..6, Keyboard's `baseOctave` to -1..8 and `transpose` to ±127.

## Repro

An Analog node with a note sequencer, `i filterType 4` (SVF LP36) and `f resonance 1`. Peak 26. At resonance 0.5 the peak is 3.1; the LP24 peak is 1.3.

## What the sweep did

The mod sweep drives every control at once. The Analog row peaked at 36. Removing any one key left it hot except `resonance` and `filterType`; single-key runs peaked at 0.6 or less.

## Why it is not fixed

It is the filter's resonant peak at the top of the knob. The output is finite. A fix (gain compensation or a soft clip) changes the sound of every saved patch that uses a high resonance. With few users that is a worse trade than a loud knob position. If the owner wants it, the place is the SVF's resonance-to-Q map.

## Other HOTAUDIO rows in the same sweep

Dynamics, EQ, Saturator, Transient Shaper and Wavetable Shaper under a bipolar LFO are the output or makeup gain knob at its +12 dB (or +24 dB) maximum. This is the Saturator case already recorded in CHECKPOINT.

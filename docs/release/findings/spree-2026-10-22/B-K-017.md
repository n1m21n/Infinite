## Repro

1. Load a Field Notes patch holding `i root 2147483647`: `2147483647 + 60` overflows in MusicTime::DegreeToNote (MusicTime.h:411), on the audio thread (SampleRuntime.h:135).
2. In Field Notes code, call `deg()` with a huge value or NaN, or index a table with NaN. `(int)std::floor(x)` and `(int)regs[...]` are undefined outside int's range.

## Expected

Notes come out of a valid key. Any float a program computes gives a defined degree or index.

## Actual

UBSan reports signed overflow on the audio path. On arm64 the casts happen to saturate, but on other targets the result is unspecified.

## Fix note

- **Load:** root wraps to its pitch class, and an unknown scale plays as major.
- **Deg:** clamps the degree to ±1000 before the cast (that is about 140 octaves, far past MIDI). NaN gives 0.
- **Table load and store:** the index is clamped as a float, and NaN gives 0. The result is the same as before for every in-range value.

All 8 field self-tests pass.

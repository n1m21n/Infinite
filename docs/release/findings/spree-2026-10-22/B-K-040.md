## Repro

A patch with `node 1 Synths Equation Synth` and a `formula` param of 50000 "(" (the sweep's `parens` string). `Infinite --audio-summary patch.inf out.json`: exit -11.

## Expected

A formula that can't be parsed is an error shown on the node. The node keeps its last good formula.

## Actual

`Parser::ParseExpression` -> `ParsePrimary` recurses once per "(", with no limit. The same holds for call arguments and prefix chains (`-----...`, `!!!...`).

A second shape of the same bug: `1+1+1+...` (200000 terms) parses without recursing, but builds an AST as deep as it is long. `Evaluate` and the destructor recurse down it and overflow.

## Fix

A depth guard on `ParseExpression` and `ParseUnary` (200 levels), and a 4096-character cap in `Parse`. Both give a parse error and leave the node on its last good formula.

## Not affected

Formula, Field Modifier, Field Primitive, Field Effect and Sketch got the same strings and returned handled errors (E_FORMULA_COMPILE, E_FIELD_COMPILE, E_RENDER). `expr` lines with 50000 "(" or "-" also return a handled error.

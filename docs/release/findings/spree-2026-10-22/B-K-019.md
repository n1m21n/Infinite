## Repro

Load a patch holding an extreme int in one of these, then draw a frame or render audio:
- `i octave` or `i semi`, or a Wavetable engine's `.octave` or `.semi`.
- Mixer `i channels 100000`.
- Drum Sequencer `i rate 100000`.

## Expected

The node keeps its control ranges.

## Actual

- **Six synths** (Oscillator, Wavetable, Equation, Metallic, Wave Terrain, Spectral Synth):
  - The bodies compute `octave + 4` to index OctaveNames, which overflows at INT_MAX (OscBodies.cpp:1478 and others).
  - The audio thread computes `note + octave * 12 + semi`, which overflows at INT_MIN (WavetableSynthCore.h:648, MetallicNode.cpp:139, and others).
  - At 100000 the pitch goes to inf, so the phase goes NaN, and SampleBank's `(int)x` is UB (EquationDsp.h:77, WaveTerrainDsp.h:383).
- **Mixer:** `AudioInputSlot(slot)` checks `slot < numChannels` only. When the wiring code counts pins it reads `inputs[12]` and past it (AudioNodes.h:84, Wiring.cpp:422). A patch cable to slot 13 or higher would write there.
- **Drum Sequencer:** `(RateDivision)rate` loads an invalid enum value (MusicTime.h:57, DrumSequencerNode.cpp:767). Spectral Synth has the same latent bug in BPM-sync scan mode.

## Fix note

- **Clamps after load:** octave to -4..4 and semi to -12..12 (the dropdown ranges); Mixer channels to 0..12 (the stepper range); rate to 0..kNumRateDivisions-1 (as Drum Sequencer's own groove copy at line 1047 already does).
- **Phase guard:** both SampleBanks reset a non-finite phase to 0.
- **Not changed:** the other `(RateDivision)` casts in the FX kernels and MPC already clamp their source.

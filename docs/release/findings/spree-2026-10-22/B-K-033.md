## Repro

Load a patch with an Oscillator set to `f volume 1e9`, or a Wavetable set to `f a.volume 1e9`, and play it.

## Expected

Volume is held to 0..1, as the knob does.

## Actual

`AudioWavetableNode` multiplies by the smoothed engine volume with no clamp (`sm.eng[e].volume` into the voice gain), so the output is about 1e9. Position, warp and resonance are clamped at the read, but volume, pan, stereo width, the envelopes, pitch and filter amounts, and the globals are not.

The first sweep flagged Oscillator but not Wavetable. Both share the engine; the difference is only that Oscillator free-runs in the fuzz rig.

## Fix note

`WavetableEngine::ClampToRange()` holds every engine float to its knob's range. Wavetable calls it per engine from `VisitParams` and clamps its globals (mix, volume, frequency, glide, pitchBend, fmDepth). Oscillator does the same for its one engine and its globals, and also enforces its sync knob's floor of 1.

Blast radius:
- All factory presets for both nodes are inside the ranges.
- The test writers (SynthAudit, DspFixtures) set members directly, so they are unaffected.

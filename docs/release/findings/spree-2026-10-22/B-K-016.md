## Repro

Load a patch holding `i stopCount 100000` (Color Ramp), `i bandCount 100000` (Audio Color Ramp) or `i activeChannel 2147483647` (Curves), then draw a frame (`--frame`).

## Expected

The node draws with its count or channel inside its arrays.

## Actual

- **Color Ramp:** the body loops `stopPos[i]` to stopCount, so index 32 is out of bounds for float[32] (ParamBodies2.cpp:762). With a negative stopCount, AddStop writes `stopPos[stopCount++]`, which is out of bounds.
- **Audio Color Ramp:** the body loops `crossoverPos[b]` to bandCount, so index 7 is out of bounds for float[7] (GeometryParams.cpp:1585). The clamp at line 1453 is local only.
- **Curves:** `Shape(activeChannel)` returns `mShapes[activeChannel]` unchecked (CurvesNode.h:41). DrawCurveEditor and ResetChannel use it, and ResetChannel writes.

The cook paths already clamped. Only the bodies and edit helpers trusted the loaded value.

## Fix note

The node clamps after loading: stopCount to 1..32, bandCount to 2..8 (the same range the cook uses), and activeChannel to 0..3. All 8 fuzz repros render, rc 0.

## Repro

Load a patch with an Analog node whose floats are past their knob ranges, for example `f volume 1e9`, `f resonance 1e9` or `f drive 1e9`. Let it free-run.

## Expected

Every param is held to its knob's or dropdown's range.

## Actual

`AnalogSynthCore` clamps fm, detune, oscMix and the derived frequencies. Volume, the osc levels, sub, noise, resonance, drive, keyTrack, pw1 and the envelope reach the voice unclamped. The output goes NaN or very loud. B-K-026 drops 1e30 at load, but 1e9 passes.

## Fix note

`AnalogNode::VisitParams` clamps each float to its knob range, and oct/semi to their dropdown ranges (-4..4 and -24..24). This covers file load, OSC, RPC, clipboard and presets. All 10 Analog factory presets are inside the ranges.

The header comment says oct is -3..3, but the dropdown offers -4..4, so the clamp follows the dropdown.

## Notes

## Repro

`Infinite --frame <patch> 0.5 <dir>/` on Torus Knot (image cable from Formula or Ramp) > Mesh to Faces > Render 3D, run 4 times: 4 different frame_00015.png. Patches: /tmp/chainfuzz3/fail/s7-80, 188, 229, 420, 543, 595 (6 of 600 fuzz chains, seed 7).

## Actual

About 2,500 scattered pixels differ by a few levels. With mipmapping bypassed in PrepareSurfaceTexture (src/nodes/Geometry3DNodes.cpp) 4 runs are identical; with only anisotropy off they still differ, so the cause is the LINEAR_MIPMAP_LINEAR sampling of the surface texture on point-sprite draws. Why it varies run to run is not known.

## Notes

Cosmetic, no crash or NaN. Not fixed: plain linear filtering would change the look of textured sprites. Listed in the v0.6 release notes as a known issue; Mesh to Points, Mesh to Edges and Mesh to Faces are in chain_fuzz's known-nondeterministic list.

Update: root cause was implicit-derivative texture sampling after a divergent discard (round sprite corners); mip data itself was identical across runs. Fixed by taking UV derivatives at the top of main() and sampling with textureLod. 4 identical frames on all 6 failing chains. textureGrad was tried and the macOS driver ignores its gradients. The triplanar path for sprites is unchanged. The fuzzer exception was reverted.

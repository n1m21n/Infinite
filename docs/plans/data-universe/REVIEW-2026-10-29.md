## Cause found in the code (2026-10-29)
Every voice and effect is a random knob draw: `Seeded()` in `SampleStyles.cpp` sets about 35% of a node's knobs (variation 0.5) to a uniform
value across the whole range (log-uniform for frequencies), then picks 1-3 effects from the card (Reverb, Audio Filter, Drive, Saturator ...)
with knobs drawn the same way. A cutoff drawn log-uniform over 20 Hz-20 kHz lands under 1 kHz about half the time (dark); random Drive and
Saturator amounts distort; stacked random effects smear (muddy). No part has a designed sound: that is why leads are not massive, toms sound
like bass and textures sound like grumbly bass.

Data already on disk and barely used: Groove MIDI (1,150 files, drummer-labelled genres: rock 281, hip-hop 91, funk 77, punk 58, New Orleans
funk 48, jazz 46, Afro-Cuban, Brazilian, reggae ...) gives 916 accepted bars today, because `public_drums.py` reads only 2 bars per file
(`--per-file 2`; the performances run dozens of bars) and keeps hits as x/. with velocity and timing dropped. Chords are not a gate loss:
the 11,700 files are 965 distinct progressions, each copied into many keys, so more harmony needs a new source (Chordonomicon, POP909 chords).

## Found while measuring (2026-10-29): every part but synths, bass and textures was silent
The master's 30 Hz high-pass (WIP b1cfb419) wrote its filter type as `i type 4`. Effect params are floats, so the reader
looks for `f type`, never finds it and keeps the default: a 24 dB low-pass at 30 Hz on the whole loop. Pads, keys,
leads, drums and the rest came out near -100 dBFS and failed the master's loudness gate on every redraw; synths, bass
and textures passed only as a 40-80 Hz sine. The live website wasm predates that commit and is not affected. Fixed
(`f type`), plus the same slip on three FX band-passes and the master filter's unpinned knobs (Add() draws any knob
left out; a drawn envAmount sweeps the cutoff). Delays set by hand now turn `sync` off: with it on, `timeMs` is ignored.

## Vital voice bank (2026-10-30)
Owner: "data is gold and a slight variation can give many results". `tools/universe/vital_voice_bank.py` turns the
Vital presets in musical_datasets into 577 Wavetable voices (`GenerateSamplesVoices.inc`): Bass 153, Keys 145, Lead
125, Pad 82, Sequence 54, Percussion 18. Each oscillator's wave is matched to the nearest Infinite table by harmonic
spectrum; unison, spread, interval, level, filter 1 with its envelope sweep and the amp envelope are kept. Most Vital
presets sit on a basic saw, sine or square, so the bank's variety is in stacks, filters and envelopes more than in tables.

| Where | Share of takes | Guardrails |
|---|---|---|
| Card bass (full mix) | 50% | cutoff 150-3500 Hz, unison 4 at most, release 120 ms at most |
| Synths motif | 40% (Keys / Sequence presets) | melodic sweep peaks at 2.5 kHz or more and settles at 1.5 kHz or more; a sine or triangle engine goes an octave up |
| Leads | 50% (Lead presets) | the same; attack 60 ms at most |
| All | | resonance 0.6 at most; band-pass becomes a low-pass; nothing more than an octave under the note; ±25-30% jitter on spread, cutoff and decays |

**Diva (2026-10-30).** The same tool reads `diva_analog_presets` with `--diva`: 4,105 labelled presets with renders
(Keys 1,312, Sequence 989, Bass 859, Pad 526, Lead 419; roles from name codes such as BS, LD, KY, PD, PL). Diva's five
oscillator models use different knobs, so each row is fitted to the preset's real render at C3 instead: wave and
low-pass by nearest 32-band shape (bands under 90 Hz left out, because the renders carry rumble there), octave from the
tune knob, amp envelope from the measured attack, decay and held level. The rows are in the bank (`src 1`), but the
generator does not draw them yet: they measured darker (table above). Two limits: 32 bands cannot tell a saw from a
square once the filter closes (98% fit as saw), and a fitted cutoff is the render's 3-second average, not the sweep.
Next, if wanted: render the bank voice in Infinite and fit it to the Diva render (closed loop), instead of the
analytic spectrum.

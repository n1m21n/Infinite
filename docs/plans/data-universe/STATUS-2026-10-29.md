## 3. Words for a sound (decided today, `docs/DECISIONS.md`)

## 4. Knob settings -> sound (the core model)
The 0.21 figure is the one-model-for-all-engines MLP on whole presets. The per-engine model with model-chosen renders (active learning)
does much better: Analog group split went from 0.298 to 0.686 in one round of 2,000 renders, Oscillator from 0.192 to 0.551.
Bar: each characteristic R2 >= 0.6 on held-out banks.
**What it needs:** 2-3 active-learning rounds of 2,000 renders per main engine (7 engines), stopping when two rounds gain < 0.02.
That is about 30,000-40,000 renders, roughly 15-20 min per round in a release build, ~6-7 hours in total, one engine at a time
(overnight is right). Expected: every main engine past 0.6 on the group split; the single cross-engine model then trains on the union.
**Set up:** `tools/universe/overnight_rounds.py` runs it all (baseline P3, then up to 3 rounds per engine, stop rule, `~/STOP_OWN`,
one engine failing never stops the night). Start it from the repo, nothing else rendering:
`cd tools/universe && nohup python3 overnight_rounds.py > ~/Infinite/training-data/models/overnight/run.out 2>&1 &`
Read the result in the morning: `~/Infinite/training-data/models/overnight/<date>.jsonl` (one line per engine and round: head, gain).

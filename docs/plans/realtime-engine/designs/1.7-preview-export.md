# 1.7 Preview export (waiting for owner)

PLAN 1.7: "Preview export on the live path", gate a 1-minute 1080p film in ≤ 15 min (0.63 s/frame).
It adds an export choice, so it is an owner row: this is the design, nothing is built.

## What it is

| Export | Path | Per frame (boat, 1080p, M2) | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Final (today) | live with 24 warm-ups, or settle 16 spp denoised | 1.71 s live / 2.06 s settle | the film you post |
| Preview (new) | live with N warm-ups per frame | see table below | watch the cut now |

## Measured (boat, warm-ups per exported frame, FLIP vs 96 warm-ups)

| Warm-ups | ≈ s/frame | FLIP vs 96 | 1-minute film |
|---|---|---|---|
| 4 | ≈ 0.35 | not measured (> 0.04) | ≈ 8 min |
| 8 | ≈ 0.62 | 0.041 | ≈ 15 min |
| 12 | ≈ 0.90 | 0.029 | ≈ 22 min |
| 24 (final) | 1.71 | 0.015 | ≈ 41 min |

Per-render GPU cost is 69 ms after 1.4. 8 warm-ups is the largest count that meets the 0.63 s gate.
The gap is GI and reflection history (temporal accumulation), so it shows as grain in the bounce light, not as
wrong colours or missing shadows.

## Decision I'd take

- One export setting, "Quality: Preview / Final", default Final. Preview = 8 warm-ups.
- No new node or parameter on Render 3D; it lives in the export dialog beside the codec.
- Headless: `--quality preview`.

## Owner questions

None that block it; it needs the go-ahead because it adds a choice to the export dialog.

Slide 8

From 256 numbers to 5 rhythms

Goal: Answer the question: how do raw numbers become rhythms?

Picture: Flat pipeline illustration: raw wiggle line, then clean (hum and drift removed), 1-second window, spectrum bars, five band bars (delta to gamma) with Hz ranges, then five 0 to 1 meters.

Facts: 256 numbers per second per sensor. Last 256 numbers = 1 second = 1 Hz slots. Welch spectrum. Add up energy in each band. Band edges to confirm in BciDspEngine.cpp first.

Prompt
Build Slide 8 only. Flat illustration (PIL, Mist panel, Signal and ink, no Ember). Title 'From numbers to rhythms'. Three short text columns: Clean, Measure, Name the rhythm. Confirm exact band edges in src/core/bci/BciDspEngine.cpp before writing Hz values. Preview, update guide, stop.

Facts: Alpha eyes-closed rise: Muse behind ears 1.44x; forehead near 1x; gel caps much higher (pull exact gel values from the experiment output before drawing).

Prompt

Slide 11

Experiment 2: the 72-second calibration

Goal: Show what calibration is and what it buys.

Picture: Timeline strip: 30 s eyes open, 30 s eyes closed, 12 s five blinks (= 72 s). Below it, bars: one fixed rule 68% (50% on a later day), personal calibration 88.5% same day, 85.5% next day.

Facts: Shrinkage LDA on 16 features (4 bands x 4 sensors), 6 s smoothing, split-half test; next-day uses re-centred midpoint. 2 s smoothing drops to 73.7%.

Prompt
Build Slide 11 only. Left: the 72 s timeline illustration. Right: accuracy bars (68, 50, 88.5, 85.5). Plain words: 'fits you, not an average person'. Mention it was tested on one public Muse dataset. Stop for approval.

Results, honestly

Goal: Say what works and what does not.

Picture: Two columns: Works (speed 0.10 ms, nothing hand-weighted, clip readability 73% spectrogram, 86% sound, 92% calibrated strip) and Fails today (uncalibrated Muse alpha, single-blink switch 78% cued and 60 to 65% reading, about 1 false trigger a minute).

Prompt
Build Slide 12 only. Two aligned columns, green-free (ink and Signal only): 'Works' and 'Does not work yet'. Each row one plain sentence plus its number. Stop for approval.

Rules for every slide

White background, margins 0.65 in, no subtitles or page numbers, flat illustrations (never slanted shots), only two text colours (ink and Signal), Ember at most once, no outlines on cards, no em dashes, plain English. Update PRESENTER_GUIDE.md with Say (simple) and Go deeper for each slide.

# Website design system

How infinitedevices.org applies the brand book (BRAND.md). One rule set for every page; the hygiene suite (`tools/hygiene/run_all.py`) checks most of it. Tokens live in `website/style.css` and `website/brand.css`.

Motto: inventive and intuitive. Inventive is the patch-cable, slab and spring language. Intuitive is that every control looks and sizes the same wherever it appears.

## Foundations

| Area | Rule | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Type | Geist for text, Geist Mono for labels and code, one Caveat word per section. Steps 11.5, 13.5, 16, 18, then fluid h3/h2/h1. Sentence case, British spelling. | `--fs-*` |
| Measure | Body text at most 72ch. | `main :is(p, li)` |
| Spacing | 4px grid. Fluid values go through `round(clamp(...), 4px)`. Card padding 24 (20 on phones), section gaps 48 to 80. | all CSS |
| Radius | Cards 24 (20 phones), media 16, controls are pills (999). No outlines. | `--r-*` |
| Colour | Midnight, Mist, Paper modes. One Ember accent per frame, Signal for structure. | `brand.css` |
| Motion | Durations 125/188/333/500/667 ms only. Easing is `--ease-out`, `--ease-io` (large travel) or `--ease-in` (exits). Springs for slabs and presses. Reduced motion zeroes the durations. | `--dur-*`, `--spring-*` |

## Components

- **Pills** (chips, tag links, tabs, copy buttons): one geometry, 40px tall (44 on phones), 16px side padding, 13.5px Geist 500, `nowrap`. A 16px brand glyph may sit left of the label.
- **Buttons** (`.btn`): the larger tier, 44px, pill radius. One primary per frame. The mobile sheet has a full-width Download.
- **Cards**: surface `--card`, radius 24, shadow-1, one title, at most two lines of body. Library cards: label above, preview, one sentence, price pill left and action pill right.
- **Lists**: no bullets in marketing sections. Use numbered cards (`01`), glyph rows or pills. Prose lists in blog posts keep a muted marker.
- **Categorisation**: pills with a glyph. Audience glyphs: artists family-compositing, musicians family-notes, VJs perform, creative coders edit, developers cube.
- **Navigation**: a dock. Phones get a bottom bar, desktop an icon rail that opens into labels on hover, tap or arrow keys. Stops are Home, For you, Explore, Learn; each opens a small sheet of that group's pages, with the current page a filled pill. Download lives in the page content (hero or CTA), not in the dock.
- **Links**: inline links in the accent ink with an underline on hover; link-like controls are pills, never bare text with arrows.
- **Iconography**: glyphs from `art/icons/src`, 20px grid, 1.5 stroke, always beside a label, inlined with `class="ic"`.
- **Calls to action**: one verb, sentence case (Download Infinite, Copy code, Full screen). Never two lines.

## Page types

- **Home**: hero with live shader, one primary action, sections each with a slab heading and one Caveat word.
- **Audience pages** (generated by `gen_audiences.py`): plain display h1, short lead (under 30 words), "What you get" in three columns, nodes you will patch first, one interactive demo, "Start with" pills, "Everyone else" pills.
- **Philosophy**: long-form; sections are numbered cards. Glyphs, not hand words, for the audience list.
- **Blog**: 72ch prose, table of contents, related cards, one call to action at the end.
- **Library**: template cards in a 4 column grid (2 on tablet, 1 on phones).
- **Embeds**: one card per widget, Copy code and Full screen pills.

## Interactive and WebGL

Shaders run only while visible, pause under reduced motion and fall back to a still frame. Each demo has one control group. Controls use the pill geometry. Pages have a transfer budget of 1.8 MB before the optional 3D model viewer loads.

## Card pictures

Blog and library cards share one picture standard, built in CSS only. Never bake, generate or crop-to-poster a card image.

- The card is a gradient of its category colour (`--cat`) over a dark base. The source picture sits inside it, framed: 14px side inset, 16px radius, soft shadow, 16:10, centred above the title zone.
- The grid (bento) view shows the same picture full bleed.
- Sources are at least 720px wide and 16:10, with no text baked in. Keep the subject clear of the top chip row and the bottom title bar.
- Titles sit on a glass bar at the foot, two lines at most. Chips are glass. The card itself is solid.

## Not yet covered (candidates)

Forms (the sign-up field), empty and loading states, focus and keyboard order, the sound design of the demos, and the voice of copy. Each should get a section here before it is changed.

## UI audit
`tests/website/ui_audit.mjs` (puppeteer-core, system Chrome) loads every page at phone and desktop sizes and fails on overlapping controls, clipped dropdowns, dead clicks (controls that do nothing), look-alike non-interactive pills and footer collisions. Run: `cd tests/website && npm install && node ui_audit.mjs [page.html ...]`.

## Device scale
`website/scale.css` (loaded last on every page by `sync_chrome.py`) is the one place that sets control and type size per device. Desktop: pill 40, button 44. Phones step down one notch: pill 36, button 40, 13px labels, 12px side padding, 18px glyphs. `tests/website/ui_scale.mjs` fails when a pill or button leaves those ranges, when a phone control spans most of the screen, and when moving art (WebGL canvases, philosophy bubbles) covers a headline, caption or control. Glass labels over art are allowed. Run: `cd tests/website && node ui_scale.mjs [page.html ...]`.

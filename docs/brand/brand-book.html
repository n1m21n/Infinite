01 · Principles

Calm, premium, a little playful

Two grounds, one accent, rounded everything, no outlines. Loudness comes from the type, never from colour noise.

Two groundsMidnight and Paper. Never pure black or white.
One accentCoral on dark, terracotta on paper.
One cardRadius 24, media 16, no outline.

03 · Type

Four families, no fifth

Geist

Headlines and UI text. 700 to 800 for display, tracking -0.02 to -0.035 em.

Geist Mono

Machine text: values, labels, code, coordinates. Tabular figures.

Caveat

The one handwritten word per scene. 700, accent colour, hand underline, at most 1.5x the neighbouring text.

Instrument Serif

Rare editorial accent, italic. Never alongside Caveat in the same line.

DisplayConnection is the pointclamp(2.4rem, 7vw, 5rem) / 800
HeadingConnection is the pointclamp(1.6rem, 3.4vw, 2.4rem) / 700

04 · Shape and depth

Rounded, concentric, borderless

Card24 pxEvery card: node card, white panel, film frame, step. 20 on phones.
Media16 pxAnything inside a card. Card radius minus its 8 px padding. 12 on phones.
Chip10 pxSmall boxes: code chips, tags.
ControlpillButtons, chips, toggles, inputs: always pill.

Media radius is the card radius minus the card padding (24 − 8 = 16). Depth comes from surface value, a lit top edge and soft stacked shadows.
